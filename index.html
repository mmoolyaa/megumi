Hermes

A personal AI assistant running on a private home server.

Hermes is a self-hosted assistant used by a single person — its owner. It connects to the owner's own Google account to help with day-to-day organisation.

  • Reads Gmail messages (read-only — it cannot send, modify, or delete mail)
  • Reads and manages Google Calendar events

All data stays on the owner's own hardware. Nothing is uploaded to a third-party service, and nothing is shared or sold.
